# System logo

The original Student Grade system emblem combines an open book, graduation cap, and rising performance chart in forest green, cream, and gold. It identifies this application; it is not the official seal of Isabela State University.

Asset: [student-grade-logo.png](../assets/student-grade-logo.png), PNG with transparency. Created with the built-in image-generation tool on November 7, 2026. The original generated file was copied into the project; no external asset URL is required.

The shared layout and reports use it when no custom school logo has been uploaded. Administrators can continue to supply their approved school logo through Settings. The former placeholder asset is retained for compatibility.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: an original square app emblem for Student Grade & Performance Analysis DDS, a PHP academic grade portal with a forest-green, warm cream, restrained gold interface. Create one polished, memorable flat logo: an open book whose clean geometric pages subtly form a rising academic-progress motif, with a small scholarly cap integrated above the book. Use a simple balanced silhouette, thick clear shapes, very limited detail, generous clean edges, legible at 32 pixels. Palette: deep forest green #173e32 and muted gold #e2c36f, with a warm cream #f5f4e9 disc or shield behind the mark so it remains visible on both dark green and white UI. Background outside this compact emblem must be genuinely transparent alpha. Center the emblem and let it occupy about 88% of the square. No words, initials, letters, numbers, university seal, official ISU insignia, gradients, photographic effects, shadows, 3D, mockup, border frame, watermark, or extra variants. This is the system's own identity and must not imply an official university seal.
